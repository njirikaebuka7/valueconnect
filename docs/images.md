# Image brief

> **Status:** all 27 images below are installed in `public/images` (supplied by the owner, October 2026). Replace a file with the same name to change a photo. Sample-listing photos have their own brief in `docs/property-images.md`.

Every photo slot on the website, what it should show, and the size to supply.

## Rules for every image

- **Real photographs only**: no AI images, illustrations, watermarks or text on the image.
- **Licence:** use your own photos, or free-for-commercial-use stock from **Unsplash** or **Pexels**. Don't use Pinterest, Google Images or other websites; those photos belong to someone else.
- **Format:** JPG, at least the size shown (bigger is fine; the site resizes automatically). Keep each file under 5 MB.
- **Name each file exactly as in the "File name" column**, so it drops straight into place.
- **Setting:** prefer Lagos and Nigeria, with Nigerian people in the people shots. Lekki, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Eko Atlantic and Abuja all fit.

These sit behind the big headlines, darkened with a navy tint so white text stays readable. Choose wide shots with plenty of sky or open space in the middle. Busy detail behind the headline gets lost under the tint.

## 5. Property listings

You don't need to supply these here. Each listing's photos are uploaded in **Admin → Properties**, and the site resizes them automatically.

For the best results:
- landscape, at least 1600 × 1200
- the first photo is the cover
- 4–10 photos per listing: front view, living room, kitchen, bedrooms, compound
