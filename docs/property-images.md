# Property photo prompts

Photos for the 16 sample listings. The first 9 are the ones shown on the **homepage**.

## The basics

- **Size:** landscape **4:3** (for example 1600 × 1200). If your tool only offers presets, choose 4:3 or 3:2 landscape.
- **File names:** `<listing-name>-1.jpg`, `-2.jpg`, `-3.jpg`, `-4.jpg`, using the listing names in bold below.
  - Photo **1** is the cover shown on the listing card, so make it the strongest exterior shot.
- **Must-have:** photo 1 for every listing (16 images). Photos 2–4 fill each listing's gallery.
- **How to send:** put them all in a folder called `properties`, zip it and attach it here, as you did with the last batch.
- **Every prompt already ends with the style line**, so you can copy each one as it is:
  > photorealistic real-estate photograph, Nigeria, natural daylight, true-to-life colours, sharp detail, wide-angle 24mm, no people, no text, no watermark

### What makes them look Nigerian (already worked into the prompts)

- **Lekki / Ikoyi / VI:** contemporary duplexes in cream or white render with stone cladding and black aluminium windows. Interlocking paving stones, a high perimeter fence with a sliding gate, a gatehouse, a BQ, a generator house, palm trees and bougainvillea.
- **Abuja:** wide streets, green hills and Aso Rock-style granite outcrops in the distance.
- **Port Harcourt / Enugu / Ibadan:** large compounds with mature mango and almond trees; slightly older but well-kept houses.
- **Land:** red laterite soil, concrete survey beacons, a fenced plot on a graded estate road.

## A note on generated photos

These listings are samples to make the site look complete at launch. When you add real listings in **Admin → Properties**, use real photos of the actual property. Clients expect the photos to show the property they will visit, and a generated photo of a real listing would mislead buyers.
